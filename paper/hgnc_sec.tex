\subsection{Gene families (HGNC): the depletion is not a zinc-finger artefact; coverage gate failed}
Large, dosage-tolerant families such as the C2H2 zinc fingers (many with long 3$'$UTRs) could make a candidate set look unconstrained. We used HGNC gene groups~\cite{hgnc} (19297 approved protein-coding symbols, 763 C2H2-ZNF; \texttt{hgnc\_families}, committed before running; 43 miRNAs; family size $=\log_2(1+\text{largest group size})$). The parse gate passed (C2H2-ZNF genes are 7.1$\times$ more frequent on chr19, bar 3$\times$), but the coverage gate failed: 88.7\% of universe symbols matched current HGNC protein-coding symbols (bar 90\%). A post-hoc audit (not pre-registered) found that 1311 of the 1974 unmatched symbols are previous HGNC symbols and 195 are approved non-protein-coding loci.
