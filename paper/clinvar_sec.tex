\subsection{ClinVar pathogenic-variant genes: the positive-control gate failed}
The last label was ClinVar (gene summary dated September 23, 2026): genes with at least five Pathogenic/Likely pathogenic alleles (5,205 genes; \texttt{clinvar\_pathogenic}, committed before running). We noted in advance that allele counts grow with coding length and testing effort. Prevalence passed (21.8\%), but the positive-control gate failed: TargetScan conserved top-200 sets do not hold significantly more PLP genes than random UTR genes (21/15, $p=0.203$). Because the label does not recover the known conserved-target signal, we treat the CNN results below as uninterpretable and do not count them for or against the finding.
